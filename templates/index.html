How It Works

  1. Upload a photo or capture it live with your camera.
  2. AI model analyzes the image and identifies the plant species.
  3. Receive detailed information with confidence scores.
  4. Optionally, download results for research or documentation.

Features

📷 Upload images or scan plants live using your device camera.
🤖 Accurate AI-powered plant detection with confidence scores.
📱 Mobile-friendly, works directly in the browser (HTTPS required for camera).
📊 View raw detection results for research and verification.
⚡ Fast processing powered by Roboflow inference API.

Why Use MangroveID?

MangroveID helps researchers, students, and coastal enthusiasts quickly identify mangroves and other coastal plants. It saves time, improves data accuracy, and supports environmental awareness.

  • Instant plant recognition
  • Data collection for research & conservation
  • Supports environmental education and awareness
